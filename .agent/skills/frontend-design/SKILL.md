# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity that is not mistaken for anyone else's. This client has already rejected proposals that felt cliché or templated, and is paying for a distinctive point of view: make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief, and take aesthetic risk if justified.

## Ground your designs in the subject matter

If the brief does not identify what the product or subject matter is, identify it yourself before designing, and confirm with the client. You can come up with one concrete subject, the design's audience, and the design's primary job, as a proposal. If there's any information in your memory about the client's preferences or context about what they're building, use that as a hint. The subject's industry, subject matter, materials, and vernacular are where distinctive visual choices come from — a design for an artisanal fashion boutique will be very aesthetically different from a high-volume sneaker drop store or a SaaS admin console. Build with the brief's real content and subject matter throughout.

## The design planning phase

Before writing production code, write a compact design plan:

1. **Named Palette (4 to 6 Hex Values)**:
   - Avoid generic AI defaults (such as cliché purple-indigo neon gradients on dark mode, or washed-out uninspired grays).
   - Establish dominant surface tone, elevated card tone, rich text tone, subtle border tone, and 1-2 intentional, authentic brand accent colors.
2. **Typography Roles & Scale**:
   - Establish a deliberate typographic hierarchy: Display/Headings, Body, Numerics, and Microcopy.
   - Use tabular numbers (`font-mono` or `tabular-nums`) for currency, stock counts, order numbers, and dates.
3. **Layout Concept & Structure**:
   - Ground layouts in physical, authentic retail and editorial structures.
   - Use intentional asymmetry, authentic product badge tags, clear visual pacing, and breathable whitespace.
4. **Anti-Cliché Self-Review**:
   - Review against predictable AI patterns:
     - No cookie-cutter cards with generic drop shadows.
     - No standard 3-column feature grids with stock icons.
     - No empty marketing fluff without real product substance.
     - No harsh, unpolished contrast lines.

## Guidelines for E-Commerce & Retail

- **Tactile, High-Credibility Product Display**:
  - Clear pricing with real installment details (e.g. "ou 12x de R$ ... sem juros").
  - Discrete badges for "Frete Grátis", "Novidade", "Desconto %", and "Estoque Limitado".
  - Clean thumbnail gallery with zoom/slide capability.
- **Frictionless, Confident Checkout**:
  - Stepper indicators that feel cohesive and lightweight.
  - Transparent payment tabs (Pix com QR Code imediato + Copia e Cola, Cartão com bandeiras reais).
  - Subtle trust badges (Criptografia 256-bit, Compra Garantida, Devolução em 7 dias).
- **Refined Microcopy**:
  - Action-oriented button labels ("Finalizar Compra Segura", "Copiar Chave Pix", "Aplicar Cupom").
  - Responsive visual states (hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading).
